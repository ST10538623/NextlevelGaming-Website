OUR STORY

Gaming is better together.

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was established in 2026 and operates in Johannesburg, South Africa. The concept was created to bring together players, friends, families and competitive gamers in a modern venue designed for immersive play and social connection.

From casual sessions to structured tournaments, the arena focuses on accessible experiences, professional support and a welcoming environment for every kind of gamer.

Our vision

To become a recognised gaming and esports destination where people can connect, compete and enjoy memorable experiences in a premium yet welcoming environment.

Our mission

To deliver accessible, high-quality gaming entertainment through modern equipment, professional support and inclusive spaces that make every visit feel exciting.

03

Our goals

Provide quality facilities, support competitive growth, deliver memorable events and build a strong community centred on friendly, high-energy play.
